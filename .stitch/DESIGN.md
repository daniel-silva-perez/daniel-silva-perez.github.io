# Minimal Blog Design System

## Brand Voice
Clean, editorial, sophisticated. A digital publication that feels thoughtfully curated with generous whitespace and typographic hierarchy.

## Color Roles
- **Primary (#2563EB)**: Links, CTAs, interactive elements — refined blue
- **Secondary (#6B7280)**: Muted text, metadata, captions — warm gray
- **Accent (#1A1A1A)**: Headlines, primary text — near black
- **Surface (#FFFFFF)**: Cards, elevated content — pure white
- **Background (#FAFAFA)**: Page background — warm off-white
- **Border (#E5E7EB)**: Subtle dividers and borders

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Plus Jakarta Sans, bold (700), tracking tight
- **Body**: Inter, regular (400), line-height 1.6 for readability
- **Captions/Meta**: Inter, medium (500), small size, gray

## Layout & Spacing
- Max content width: 1200px
- Card grid: 3 columns desktop, 2 tablet, 1 mobile
- Section padding: 80px vertical
- Card gap: 32px
- Border radius: 8px (ROUND_EIGHT)

## Component Patterns
- **Hero Card**: Full-width featured image with gradient overlay, title in white
- **Post Card**: Thumbnail top, category chip, title, excerpt, author avatar + name + date
- **Navigation**: Sticky header with blur backdrop, logo left, links right
- **Footer**: Centered, minimal links, copyright

## Elevation
- Cards: subtle shadow (0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.06))
- Header: blur backdrop with subtle bottom border
