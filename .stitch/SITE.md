# Minimal Blog — Site Vision

## 1. Overview

A clean, editorial personal blog for Danny Silva, a software engineer. The design is minimal, sophisticated, and typography-driven with generous whitespace.

## 2. Goals

- Showcase thoughtful writing on software engineering, AI, and technology
- Provide a beautiful reading experience
- Maintain a consistent editorial aesthetic across all pages

## 3. Target Audience

- Software engineers and developers
- Technology enthusiasts
- Potential employers and collaborators

## 5. Roadmap

### Next Tasks

1. **Enhance Blog Homepage** — Add newsletter subscription section, featured categories, and improved post cards with better hover interactions
